## What we make

**Q: What print methods do you offer?**
A: Screen printing, embroidery, DTF (direct-to-film), and sublimation — matched to your garment, design and quantity.
Source: /faq
Note: we do **not** offer DTG. If a customer asks about DTG, explain that DTF gives the same full-colour result on any fabric.

**Q: Which method is right for my order?**
A: Screen printing suits bulk runs with few colours. DTF handles full-colour art on any fabric. Embroidery suits logos on structured garments like polos, jackets and hats.
Source: /faq

**Q: What products can you customise?**
A: Apparel (t-shirts, polos, hoodies and sweatshirts, jackets, vests, workwear, athletic wear, youth and women's), hats, bags, promotional products (drinkware, pens, office items, tech accessories, outdoor and eco-friendly items), signs and displays (banners, banner stands, flags, indoor and outdoor signs, vehicle and window graphics, trade-show displays), print products (business cards, flyers, brochures, postcards, stickers and labels) and safety products.
Source: site navigation, /products

**Q: Do you print vinyl banners?**
A: Yes — vinyl banners, mesh banners, pole banners and double-sided banners, under Signs & Displays.
Source: /products (Signs & Displays → Banners)

**Q: Do you do embroidery on hats?**
A: Yes. Embroidery is our recommended method for hats — beanies, toques, dad hats, trucker hats, snapbacks and more.
Source: /products (Hats), /faq

**Q: Which brands do you carry?**
A: Around 75 brands including Gildan, ATC, Bella+Canvas, Carhartt, Nike, The North Face, Champion, Stormtech, Port Authority, Under Armour and OGIO. The full list is under Brands on the site.
Source: /brands

## Ordering and pricing

**Q: How is my price determined?**
A: Price is based on the garment, print method, quantity, number of ink colours and print locations. Pricing drops automatically at 50, 100 and 250+ units.
Source: /faq
Note: the assistant must not state a dollar figure unless it comes from the site's estimate or quote tools. If asked "how much for 100 shirts", explain what the price depends on and offer the instant estimate on any product page or a quote from the team.

**Q: What is the minimum order quantity?**
A: Most runs start at 24 units. Smaller runs may be possible for embroidery — ask your rep.
Source: /faq

**Q: What are my payment options?**
A: Credit card, e-transfer, or Net-30 terms for approved accounts. A 50% deposit is required on custom orders.
Source: /faq

**Q: When am I charged?**
A: Nothing is charged until you approve your proof. Checkout records a payment preference; the invoice follows proof approval.
Source: product page and cart ("Pay after proof")

**Q: How do I place an order?**
A: Choose a product, click Design to add your artwork (upload it, or describe it and let the design assistant draw it), enter your quantities by size, and check out. You'll receive a digital proof to approve before anything prints.
Source: homepage "How to order", /design

**Q: Can I reorder a past design?**
A: Yes — we keep your artwork and specs on file, so reorders are quick with no new setup fee.
Source: /faq

**Q: Do you offer discounts?**
A: Pricing drops automatically at 50, 100 and 250+ units. There are no discount codes.
Source: /faq
Note: the assistant must never invent or offer a discount code.

## Artwork and proofs

**Q: What file types do you accept for artwork?**
A: AI, EPS, PDF, or high-resolution PNG (300 DPI minimum). Not print-ready? Our team can vectorise your logo.
Source: /faq

**Q: Can I get a sample or proof before I order?**
A: Yes — every order gets a free digital proof for sign-off before it goes to press. We review your artwork and send a mockup showing colours, placement and sizing.
Source: /faq

**Q: Is every order proofed before print?**
A: Yes. Nothing goes to press until you approve the digital proof.
Source: /faq

**Q: Do you do colour matching?**
A: Yes — we match any colour using the Pantone Matching System, with 37 stock ink colours on hand.
Source: /faq

**Q: Can you design something for me?**
A: Yes. On any product's Design page you can describe what you need and the design assistant will draw a logo or badge for you to place on the garment. You can also upload your own art. Our team prepares the final file for the press.
Source: /design

## Shipping, delivery and pickup

**Q: Do you offer free shipping?**
A: Yes — orders over $300 (before tax) ship free anywhere in Canada and the United States. Under $300, shipping is calculated and confirmed after we review your order.
Source: /faq, checkout Delivery Method step

**Q: Where can you ship to?**
A: Anywhere in Canada and the United States.
Source: /faq

**Q: Can I pick up my order in person?**
A: Yes — local pickup from our Vancouver production studio. We confirm the pickup window once your proof is approved and production is complete.
Source: /shipping, checkout Delivery Method step

**Q: Do you deliver locally?**
A: Yes — courier delivery across Vancouver, Burnaby, Richmond, North Vancouver and nearby municipalities, usually next business day after production.
Source: /shipping

**Q: How long does shipping take?**
A: Canada-wide: 2–7 business days in transit by tracked ground or priority shipping. Final timing depends on destination, carton count and carrier capacity.
Source: /shipping

**Q: Do you offer drop shipping or blind shipping?**
A: *Not stated on the current site.* Until the client confirms this service, the assistant should say it will check with the team and take the customer's details.
Source: none — the old site's FAQ claimed this; the current site does not.
