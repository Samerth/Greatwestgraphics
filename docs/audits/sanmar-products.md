# SanMar products — one simple sheet

Open **`sanmar-products.csv`** in Excel or Google Sheets. That is the only file you need.

A **product** here means one garment style (for example the Allmade Organic Cotton Tee). Different sizes of the same tee are the same product. Different colours of that tee are counted under “Number of colours”.

## The short answer

You are **not missing a SanMar login**. Photos we do have are real SanMar photos.

You are **not getting every picture SanMar has**. I checked SanMar’s own public website for all 472 products.

## How to read the sheet

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Product name | The garment |
| Brand | Who makes it |
| Style number | The code on the tag (AL2100CA, ATC1000, …) |
| Number of colours | How many colours that product comes in |
| Colours with a photo | How many of those colours have a picture in our shop |
| Front / Side / Back | Whether we have that angle |
| Photo of someone wearing it | A model / on-body shot |
| Different photos in our shop | How many distinct pictures we stored |
| In plain English | One-line status |
| Checked against SanMar website | What their public page has vs us |

## What this means in practice

- Most products are fine: they have colour photos.
- Side and back are often missing. SanMar does not always publish those angles.
- **31 products** have photos on SanMar’s website that never landed in our shop. Those are the ones worth a fresh photo pull.
- Extra pictures on SanMar’s site are usually more angles or crops of the same colour. We keep one front, one side, and one back per colour on purpose.
